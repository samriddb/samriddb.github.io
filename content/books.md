## Books

Some cool books:

- **Almond** by Sohn Won-pyung - contemporary story about human nature
- **1984** by George Orwell - dystopian classic
- **Norwegian Wood** by Haruki Murakami - coming of age
- **I Am That** by Sri Nisargadatta Maharaj - self-inquiry, non-duality, nature of consciousness
- **In the Buddha's Word** - anthology of the Buddha's teachings
- **Siddhartha** by Herman Hesse - self-discovery
- **The Tragedy of Great Power Politics** by John J. Mearsheimer - international relations theory
