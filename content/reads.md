## Books

- **Almond** by Sohn Won-pyung - contemporary story about human nature
- **1984** by George Orwell - dystopian classic
- **Norwegian Wood** by Haruki Murakami - coming of age
- **I Am That** by Sri Nisargadatta Maharaj - self-inquiry, non-duality, nature of consciousness
- **In the Buddha's Word** - anthology of the Buddha's teachings
- **Siddhartha** by Herman Hesse - self-discovery
- **The Tragedy of Great Power Politics** by John J. Mearsheimer - international relations theory

## Essays

- [Pleasures](https://hackneybooks.co.uk/books/364/686/Pleasures.html) by Aldous Huxley: An essay on the nature of pleasure and human experience.
- [Why stories matter](https://www.joanwestenberg.com/p/why-stories-make-you-smarter-than-self-help-books) by Joan Westenberg
- [Style](https://stephango.com/style) by Steph Ango
- [The Law of Leaky Abstractions](https://www.joelonsoftware.com/2002/11/11/the-law-of-leaky-abstractions/) by Joel Spolsky
